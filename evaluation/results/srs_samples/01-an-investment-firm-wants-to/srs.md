## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) specifies the Investment Portfolio Management Platform for XYZ Investment Firm, written for business owners, developers, testers, and compliance reviewers.

Intended audience. Business and product owners read sections 1 and 2 and the use cases in 3.2; developers and architects read section 3 in full; testers use the acceptance criteria and the verification matrix; compliance and security reviewers read 2.4, 3.5, 3.6 and the compliance-control matrix.

### 1.2 Scope

The system will allow users to invest in mutual funds, stocks, and other financial instruments, collect users' financial goals, income, and risk preferences, suggest suitable investment options, track portfolio performance, view profits and losses, receive investment alerts, and allow financial advisors to monitor client portfolios and provide recommendations. The system will also handle exceptions and store user financial data securely.

This specification states 22 requirements: 5 stated in the scenario, 3 implied by analysis of what such a system needs, and 14 proposed from the regulations and security controls that apply to it. Section 3 gives each one with its origin.

The system will:

- Invest in Financial Instruments — The system shall allow users to invest in mutual funds, stocks, and other financial instruments; The system shall allow users to cancel or modify their investment plans.
- Set Investment Goals and Preferences — The system shall collect users' financial goals, income, and risk preferences.
- Get Investment Recommendations — The system shall suggest suitable investment options based on users' financial goals, income, and risk preferences.
- Monitor Portfolio and Provide Recommendations — The system shall allow financial advisors to monitor client portfolios and provide recommendations.
- Track Portfolio Performance — The system shall allow users to track portfolio performance, view profits and losses, and receive investment alerts.
- Meet regulatory obligations — Functions the applicable regulations require of the system, proposed by the compliance analysis rather than stated by a stakeholder.

The system will not:

- Tax planning and preparation — assumed; to be confirmed
- Retirement planning, estate planning, and other non-investment services — assumed; to be confirmed

## Appendix E — Recommended development life cycle

**Recommendation.** Recommended model: Prototype.

> New system + New product direction + Stakeholder involvement + Long-lived infrastructure system -> Prototype

The Prototype model is recommended because it fits the project's needs, including the need for a working model quickly, requirements clarification, technical risk investigation, and early feedback from real users. The project's requirements are not well-defined, and the system's interaction with users throughout the development process implies a need for early feedback and clarification of requirements. The Prototype model is the best choice for this project because it allows for early feedback, clarification of requirements, and investigation of technical risk, which are essential for this project's success.

|---|---|---|---|

Why Prototype suits this project:

- A working model quickly: This project requires a new system to be developed, and a working model will help stakeholders understand the system's capabilities and make informed decisions.
- Requirements clarified by seeing them run: The system's interaction with users throughout the development process, including collecting information, tracking performance, and receiving feedback, implies that stakeholders may need to see something working before they can say what they want.
- Technical risk investigated before committing: The project involves developing a new platform for investment, which implies a new product direction for the organization, and therefore technical risk should be investigated before committing to the project.
- Early feedback from real users: The system should collect users' financial goals, income and risk preferences before suggesting suitable investment options, and users should be able to track portfolio performance, view profits and losses, and receive investment alerts, implying that early feedback from real users is essential.

**Trade-off.** Prototype assumes scope is expected to keep changing as work goes on, and that technical feasibility itself is in doubt and has to be proven — neither is true here, so that part of its design earns nothing on this project.
