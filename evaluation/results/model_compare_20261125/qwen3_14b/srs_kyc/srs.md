## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the requirements for the Digital Customer Onboarding Platform, a system developed for a neo-bank to streamline the customer onboarding process. It is intended for use by business owners, developers, testers, and compliance reviewers.

Intended audience. Business and product owners read sections 1 and 2 and the use cases in 3.2; developers and architects read section 3 in full; testers use the acceptance criteria and the verification matrix; compliance and security reviewers read 2.4, 3.5, 3.6 and the compliance-control matrix.

It was generated by Syntheia from the problem scenario [R0] and a curated, versioned knowledge base of financial regulations; it is a draft for review, and every requirement is Pending until approved.*

### 1.2 Scope

The system supports both video KYC and document-based KYC flows for customer onboarding. It ensures PAN and Aadhaar verification, assigns risk scores, escalates high-risk customers, and completes the onboarding process within 15 minutes for low-risk customers. The system also provides real-time status tracking, automated notifications, and secure data storage. Benefits include compliance with RBI regulations, reduced onboarding time, and improved risk management.

This specification states 36 requirements: 7 stated in the scenario, 8 implied by analysis of what such a system needs, and 21 proposed from the regulations and security controls that apply to it. Section 3 gives each one with its origin.

Business objectives stated in the scenario:

- Support both video KYC and document-based KYC flows for customer onboarding (“Build a digital customer onboarding platform for a neo-bank that supports both video KYC and document-based KYC flows.”)
- Validate PAN details against the NSDL database in real-time (“PAN details should be validated against the NSDL database in real-time.”)
- Record and store video KYC sessions for a minimum of 8 years (“Video KYC sessions must be recorded and stored for a minimum of 8 years as per RBI's KYC master direction.”)
- Assign a risk score to each customer based on their profile, income declaration, and PEP screening results (“The system should assign a risk score to each customer based on their profile, income declaration, and PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening results.”)
- Escalate high-risk customers to a compliance officer for enhanced due diligence before account activation (“High-risk customers must be escalated to a compliance officer for enhanced due diligence before account activation.”)
- Complete the onboarding process within 15 minutes for low-risk customers on a stable internet connection (“The entire onboarding process should take no longer than 15 minutes for low-risk customers on a stable internet connection.”)

The system will:

- Initiate KYC Flow — Customer selects either video KYC or document-based KYC flow for onboarding.
- Upload Identity Documents — Customer uploads PAN card and Aadhaar for identity verification.
- Assign Risk Score — System assigns a risk score based on customer profile, income declaration, and PEP screening.
- Escalate High-Risk Customer — System escalates high-risk customers to a compliance officer for enhanced due diligence.
- Track Onboarding Status — Customer views real-time status tracking and detailed history of onboarding steps.
- Cancel or Correct Information — Customer cancels or corrects submitted information before final submission.
- Send automated notifications to customers via email and SMS — The system shall send automated notifications to customers via email and SMS at key stages of the onboarding process, including completion, escalation, and approval.
- Support administration of user roles and permissions, allowing compliance — The system shall support administration of user roles and permissions, allowing compliance officers to review and approve high-risk customers.
- Meet regulatory obligations — Functions the applicable regulations require of the system, proposed by the compliance analysis rather than stated by a stakeholder.

The system will not:

- Supporting other forms of identity verification beyond PAN and Aadhaar — assumed; to be confirmed
- Handling onboarding for non-individual customers such as businesses or legal entities — assumed; to be confirmed
- Providing customer support beyond automated notifications and status tracking — assumed; to be confirmed

## 2 Overall description

### 2.1 Product perspective

**Business case.** The system is new and replaces the existing manual onboarding process. It integrates with the NSDL database for PAN validation and works with external systems for video recording and storage, email and SMS services, and screen readers for accessibility.

**Operational concept.** Customers initiate the onboarding process by selecting either video KYC or document-based KYC. They upload PAN and Aadhaar documents, which are validated in real-time. For video KYC, sessions are recorded and stored for 8 years. The system assigns a risk score based on customer profile, income, and PEP screening. High-risk customers are escalated to compliance officers for review. Low-risk customers complete the process within 15 minutes. Customers receive real-time updates and can cancel or correct information before final submission.

## Appendix E — Recommended development life cycle

**Recommendation.** Recommended model: V-Shaped.

> High-priority+high-risk+regulatory+integration+long-life -> V-Shaped

V-Shaped is recommended because it aligns with the project's well-defined, high-priority, and high-risk requirements, ensuring rigorous testing and compliance with regulatory mandates. The mismatch on PTR-3 does not outweigh the strong alignment with other critical factors like integration, long-term infrastructure, and formal verification needs.

| Rank | Model | Fit | Matched / answered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | V-Shaped | 90% | 9/10 |
| 2 | Waterfall | 80% | 8/10 |
| 3 | Incremental | 70% | 7/10 |
| 4 | RAD | 50% | 5/10 |
| 5 | Spiral | 40% | 4/10 |

Why V-Shaped suits this project:

- Testing planned alongside each development stage: This ensures that regulatory compliance requirements, such as those in REQ-001 and REQ-002, are verified at each stage of development, reducing the risk of non-compliance with KYC and data privacy regulations.
- Test cases written early, from the requirements: This aligns with the high-priority and high-risk requirements like REQ-003 and REQ-004, ensuring that real-time PAN validation and long-term video storage are rigorously tested from the start.
- Defects found at the stage that caused them: This is critical for requirements like REQ-006, where high-risk customer escalation must be accurate and reliable, minimizing the chance of errors in compliance workflows.
- Every requirement traceable to a test: This supports the high regulatory and security requirements in REQ-001, REQ-002, and REQ-004, ensuring that all compliance and data management needs are fully covered by testing.

**Trade-off.** V-Shaped assumes an existing system is being extended — that is not the case here, so that part of its design earns nothing on this project.
