## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) specifies the Digital Customer Onboarding Platform for a neo-bank, written for business owners, developers, testers, and compliance reviewers.

Intended audience. Business and product owners read sections 1 and 2 and the use cases in 3.2; developers and architects read section 3 in full; testers use the acceptance criteria and the verification matrix; compliance and security reviewers read 2.4, 3.5, 3.6 and the compliance-control matrix.

### 1.2 Scope

The system will support both video KYC and document-based KYC flows, validate PAN details against the NSDL database in real-time, assign a risk score to each customer, and complete the onboarding process within 15 minutes for low-risk customers. The system will also provide a status tracking feature, notify customers and compliance officers, and store customer onboarding data for a minimum of 8 years.

This specification states 37 requirements: 7 stated in the scenario, 7 implied by analysis of what such a system needs, and 23 proposed from the regulations and security controls that apply to it. Section 3 gives each one with its origin.

Business objectives stated in the scenario:

- The entire onboarding process should take no longer than 15 minutes for low-risk customers on a stable internet connection. (“The entire onboarding process should take no longer than 15 minutes for low-risk customers on a stable internet connection.”)

The system will:

- Support both video KYC and document-based KYC flows — The system shall support both video KYC and document-based KYC flows; The system shall allow customers to upload PAN card and Aadhaar for identity verification.
- Risk Assessment and Escalation — System assesses customer risk and escalates high-risk customers
- Monitoring and Logging — Administrator monitors system performance and customer onboarding activity
- Onboarding Application Management — Customer manages onboarding application
- Meet regulatory obligations — Functions the applicable regulations require of the system, proposed by the compliance analysis rather than stated by a stakeholder.

The system will not:

- Handle customer support or account management — assumed; to be confirmed

Why Prototype suits this project:

- A working model quickly: This project requires the system to interoperate with external databases (NSDL), third-party APIs, and regulator or bureau services (RBI's KYC master direction), which implies a need for a working model to test these integrations quickly.
- Requirements clarified by seeing them run: The project involves building a digital customer onboarding platform for a neo-bank, which implies a new product direction for the organization, and the stakeholders need to see something working before they can say what they want.
- Technical risk investigated before committing: The requirement to validate PAN details against the NSDL database in real-time implies that technical feasibility is not in doubt, as this is a well-established process in the financial industry, but still requires investigation.
- Early feedback from real users: The problem statement mentions iterating based on user feedback, indicating user involvement throughout the life cycle, which implies a need for early feedback from real users.

**Trade-off.** Prototype assumes scope is expected to keep changing as work goes on, and that technical feasibility itself is in doubt and has to be proven — neither is true here, so that part of its design earns nothing on this project.
