## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This SRS specifies a digital customer onboarding platform for a neo-bank, written for business owners, developers, testers, and compliance reviewers.

Intended audience. Business and product owners read sections 1 and 2 and the use cases in 3.2; developers and architects read section 3 in full; testers use the acceptance criteria and the verification matrix; compliance and security reviewers read 2.4, 3.5, 3.6 and the compliance-control matrix.

It was generated by Syntheia from the problem scenario [R0] and a curated, versioned knowledge base of financial regulations; it is a draft for review, and every requirement is Pending until approved.*

### 1.2 Scope

The system will support video KYC and document-based KYC flows. It will allow customers to upload PAN and Aadhaar cards, validate PAN details against the NSDL database in real-time, record and store video KYC sessions for a minimum of 8 years, calculate risk scores based on profile, income declaration, and PEP screening, escalate high-risk accounts to compliance officers, complete low-risk onboarding within 15 minutes, display progress indicators, notify customers upon success, allow session cancellation with data deletion, handle NSDL timeouts, provide audit trails for compliance officers, configure risk thresholds and external services, and display accessibility-compliant UI elements.

This specification states 36 requirements: 7 stated in the scenario, 7 implied by analysis of what such a system needs, and 22 proposed from the regulations and security controls that apply to it. Section 3 gives each one with its origin.

Business objectives stated in the scenario:

- Support both video KYC and document-based KYC flows. (“[REQ-001] The system shall support two distinct customer onboarding flows: video KYC and document-based KYC.”)
- Validate PAN details against the NSDL database in real-time. (“[REQ-003] The system shall validate PAN card details against the NSDL database in real-time.”)
- Store video KYC recordings for a minimum of 8 years as per RBI's KYC master direction. (“[REQ-004] The system shall record all video KYC sessions and store the recordings for a minimum of 8 years.”)
- Assign a risk score based on profile, income declaration, and PEP screening results. (“[REQ-005] The system shall calculate and assign a risk score to each customer based on their profile, income declaration, and PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening results.”)
- Escalate high-risk customers for enhanced due diligence before account activation. (“[REQ-006] The system shall escalate accounts of customers identified as 'High-risk' to a compliance officer for enhanced due diligence before account activation.”)
- Complete the entire onboarding process for low-risk customers within 15 minutes. (“[REQ-007] The system shall complete the entire onboarding process for low-risk customers within a maximum of 15 minutes.”)

The system will:

- Initiate Onboarding Session — The customer selects a flow (video or document), uploads PAN and Aadhaar, views progress, and cancels if needed.
- Calculate Risk Score — The system calculates a risk score based on profile, income, and PEP screening results.
- Manage High-Risk Escalation — The system escalates high-risk accounts to the compliance officer for enhanced due diligence.
- Complete Onboarding and Notify — The system activates the account upon successful completion and notifies the customer via email and mobile.
- Meet regulatory obligations — Functions the applicable regulations require of the system, proposed by the compliance analysis rather than stated by a stakeholder.

The system will not:

- Account activation is contingent upon compliance officer review for high-risk customers; the system does not automatically activate high-risk accounts — assumed; to be confirmed
- The system does not manage the actual account activation process beyond escalation and notification; it relies on external processes for final activation status — assumed; to be confirmed

## 2 Overall description

### 2.1 Product perspective

**Business case.** The system is a new digital customer onboarding platform that works with the NSDL database and external PEP lists.

**Operational concept.** Customers upload PAN and Aadhaar documents. The system validates PAN details against NSDL in real-time. If validation fails due to timeout, an error message is displayed with a retry option. Video KYC sessions are recorded and stored for at least 8 years. A risk score is calculated based on profile, income declaration, and PEP screening results. High-risk customers are escalated to a compliance officer who views the full audit trail before account activation. Low-risk customers complete onboarding within 15 minutes. Upon successful completion, customers receive notifications via email and mobile number. Customers can cancel sessions at any point, triggering secure deletion of data. Administrators configure risk thresholds and external services. Accessibility-compliant UI elements are displayed during video KYC and document upload steps.

Why Waterfall suits this project:

- Predictable schedule and cost: This property supports the fixed delivery window defined in REQ-007, ensuring the 15-minute onboarding limit is met within a strict timeline.
- Clear phase gates and sign-offs: These gates facilitate formal verification against high-risk regulatory mandates like those in REQ-002 and REQ-006 before account activation.
- Documentation produced as you go: This ensures the creation of necessary records for long-term archival required by REQ-004 to store video KYC sessions for a minimum of 8 years.
- Simple to manage and staff: Simplicity aids in managing the specific functional scope of building a new digital onboarding platform as described in REQ-001 without complex iterative overheads.

**Trade-off.** Waterfall assumes the system stands on its own — that is not the case here, so that part of its design earns nothing on this project.
