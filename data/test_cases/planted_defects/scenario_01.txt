A private bank wants a mobile app for instant personal loans. Applicants sign in with their mobile number and a password, then complete KYC by entering their PAN and Aadhaar number and uploading a selfie, and share six months of bank statements. Credit scores are pulled from CIBIL in real time. The app must show a loan decision within 30 seconds of submission. Loans above 5 lakh need approval from a branch credit officer, while loans up to 10 lakh can be approved automatically by the decision engine. Applicant documents should be kept for three years and then deleted. Customer support staff must be able to see the full Aadhaar number so they can help applicants. The decision must be returned to the applicant in under 10 seconds. Users log in using their registered mobile number and a password. The bank's fraud team should be alerted to suspicious applications. The project has three developers and must launch before Diwali, in four months.
