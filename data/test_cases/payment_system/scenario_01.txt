We need a UPI-based payment gateway for merchants to accept digital payments via QR code scanning and payment links. The system must support transaction volumes of at least 10,000 payments per minute during peak hours like festive sales. Failed transactions must be auto-refunded to the customer's bank account within 30 minutes. Merchant settlements should happen on a T+1 basis with detailed transaction reports accessible via a dashboard. All payment data must be encrypted end-to-end and the system must comply with NPCI's UPI technical specifications and PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. Suspicious transactions exceeding Rs 1 lakh must be flagged for manual review by the fraud team before settlement.
