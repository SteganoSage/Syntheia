Build a digital customer onboarding platform for a neo-bank that supports both video KYC and document-based KYC flows. Customers must upload PAN card and Aadhaar for identity verification — PAN details should be validated against the NSDL database in real-time. Video KYC sessions must be recorded and stored for a minimum of 8 years as per RBI's KYC master direction. The system should assign a risk score to each customer based on their profile, income declaration, and PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening results. High-risk customers must be escalated to a compliance officer for enhanced due diligence before account activation. The entire onboarding process should take no longer than 15 minutes for low-risk customers on a stable internet connection.
